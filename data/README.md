# Data

Two corpora are used. **C2 (the recordings made for this thesis) is included here in
full. C1 (the third-party corpus every headline result is computed on) is not
redistributed** — it belongs to its original authors and is 6.4 GB. Get it from the DOI
below.

## C1 — Digital Voicing of Silent Speech (primary; all results)

The single-speaker sEMG corpus of Gaddy and Klein (2020). Every number in Chapters 4–5
is computed on this corpus.

- **Source:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.4064408
- Download, unpack, and place it at `code/data/DVSS/`, or point `dataset.data_root` in
  `code/configs/base.yaml` at wherever you unpacked it.

Expected layout (only the `.npy` EMG and `_info.json` transcripts are read — the pipeline
never touches the corpus audio):

```
code/data/DVSS/
├── closed_vocab/{voiced,silent}/<session>/<i>_emg.npy, <i>_info.json
└── open_vocab/{voiced_parallel_data,silent_parallel_data,nonparallel_data}/...
```

**Automated split generation**: When run directories are generated, each outputs the exact `train_split.csv`, `val_split.csv`, `test_split.csv` and `vocab.json` it was scored on. The protocols that generate them are reproducible code, not manual:

- Banpool / unseen-word split: `code/data/split.py`, justified in
  `code/data/thesis helpers/oov_split_justification.ipynb` (Table 10)
- Vocabulary-gradient subsets V1–V10: `code/experiments/subsets/exp2_gradient/`,
  generated by `code/experiments/05_prestudy_vocabulary_scaling_protocol_FINAL.ipynb`
  (Table 26)

Tables 5 and 9 (corpus summary and utterance durations) are recomputed from the raw
corpus by `code/data/thesis helpers/corpus_summary_tables.ipynb` — the only notebook
that needs C1 present in order to run.

## C2 — Secondary exploratory corpus (`C2_recordings/`, Appendix M)

The pilot corpus recorded for this thesis: one participant, vocalised / silent / subvocal,
Harvard Sentences plus isolated speech tokens and facial poses. Recorded on a
Cerelog ESP (8-channel sEMG, 1000 Hz, 24-bit, ADS1299).

```
C2_recordings/
├── Recordings_Poses_19_05_2026/     # v1 montage  -> activation map, Fig. 15
├── Recordings_Poses_20_05_2026/     # v2 montage  -> activation map, Fig. 18
├── Recordings_Tokens_{20,21}_05_2026/ # [CENSORED] isolated speech unit recordings
├── Recordings_Alphabet_19_05_2026/
├── Recordings_{14,19,21}_05_2026/   # sentence recordings
└── harvard_sentences.csv            # prompt list
```

Each trial is a raw `*_emg.npy` array of shape `(T, 8)` at 1000 Hz.

C2 **supports no result in the thesis body.** It appears only in Appendix M as an
exploratory observation, and the data quality was not formally validated. The recording
scripts and the visualisation notebooks that produce the activation maps are in
`code/data_collection/`.

**Caveat on Figures 15 and 18:** `visualize_poses_v2/v3.ipynb` computes exactly these RMS
activation heatmaps from the pose recordings above, but neither notebook writes the
figure to disk — the versions in the thesis were captured from the notebook output. The
montage figures themselves (13, 14, 16, 17, 19, 20) are photographs and are not
reproducible from any data.
